Aspera Connect Privacy Policy

Publisher: The GST Co · Contact: marketing@thegstco.com
Last updated: 29 August 2026

Aspera Connect is a companion app that lets your Linux PC (Aspera Connect desktop / Aspera Hub) place phone calls and sync contacts with your Android phone over your local network or our short-lived cloud relay.

What the app accesses

  • Phone / call — to place or end calls you start from the PC.
  • Contacts — to show your address book on the PC (stored on your devices).
  • Camera — optional, only to scan a pairing QR from the PC.
  • Network — to talk to your PC on LAN or the pairing relay.
  • Notifications — to keep the companion service running reliably.

What we collect

The app does not create user accounts and does not sell data. Call and contact data stay on your phone and PC. Pairing QR codes contain a random session id and secret that expire in about 10 minutes.

Cloud relay

When you use internet QR pairing, the phone and PC briefly connect to our relay (aspera-connect-relay.onrender.com) to exchange pairing and call commands. The relay does not need your Google account and is not used for advertising.

Children

Aspera Connect is for workplace use and is not directed at children under 13.
