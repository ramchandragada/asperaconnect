Full description
  Aspera Connect is the Android companion for Aspera Hub and Aspera Connect on Linux.

  Pair once with a QR code (works across any network). Then click phone numbers in Zoho or Hub — your phone places the call. Sync contacts to the PC, hang up from the desktop, and keep working without typing IPs.

  • QR pair over internet (or same Wi‑Fi)
  • Click-to-call from PC / Hub / Zoho tel: links
  • Contacts sync to Linux desktop
  • Hang up from PC
  • No Developer Options required for daily use
