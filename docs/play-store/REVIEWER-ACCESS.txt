Instructions for Google reviewers:

Aspera Connect is a workplace companion. It pairs with a Linux desktop app
(Aspera Connect / Aspera Hub) via QR code and places calls on the reviewer’s phone.

To review without a company PC:
1. Install this Android app from the testing track.
2. Open the app → allow Phone + Contacts (+ notifications).
3. Tap “Start for calls”.
4. The home screen shows waiting / IP status — that is expected.
5. Full click-to-call requires a paired Linux PC with Aspera Connect showing
   “Show QR to pair”, then Scan PC QR on the phone.

No login username/password is required for the Android app itself.
